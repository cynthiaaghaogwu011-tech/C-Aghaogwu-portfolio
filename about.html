About Me

I am a full stack developer who enjoys building fast, accessible, and visually clean web applications. On the frontend, I write semantic HTML, style with Tailwind CSS, and add lightweight JavaScript that improves the experience without adding bloat.

On the backend, I build with PHP and MySQL, and with Node.js, Express, and MongoDB. I work on things like user authentication, secure forms with CSRF protection, and JSON APIs. My work spans e-commerce platforms, admin dashboards, and internal tools for teams that need software they can rely on. I care about how a system holds up under real use as much as how it looks on day one.

I also spend time on the small details, like error messages, loading states, and empty states, which most people never notice until they're missing. I like understanding why things work, not just getting them to run, and I'm always learning and improving.

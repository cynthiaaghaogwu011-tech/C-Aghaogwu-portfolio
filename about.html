About Me

I am a frontend developer driven by the challenge of building fast, accessible, and visually stunning web applications. My approach focuses on writing semantic HTML, crafting precise custom styling with Tailwind CSS, and implementing lightweight, performant JavaScript interactions that enhance the user experience without adding bloat.

My work spans e-commerce platforms, admin dashboards internal tools for teams that need websites and software they can rely on. I care about how a system holds up under real usage as i do about how it looks on day one.

Outside client work, I spend time refining the small details like error messages, loading states and empty states that most people never notice until they're missing.
